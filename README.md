## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Bloodcrave**. This trainer enhances Bloodcrave's narrative systems by controlling progression variables, relationship development, insight checks, scene access, and daily planning. It is designed for players who want to explore alternate routes, reach character endings more easily, or view optional content without replaying the entire story.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to revisit romance routes, test different moral choices, unlock optional scenes, complete the story without repeated playthroughs, or explore Bloodcrave's branching endings at their own pace.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Bloodcrave does not use conventional free-roaming movement. Progress is controlled through menus, dialogue, scene transitions, and the daily planner. Select a location or activity, then allow the scheduled event to resolve before changing trainer options or loading another save.

### Tools & Inventory

Bloodcrave has no traditional action-game inventory system. Progress is primarily represented through story flags, relationship values, Insight, character statistics, unlocked events, and gallery content. Use the trainer's progression options instead of looking for item-based enhancements.

### World & Environment

The story takes place across more than twenty locations connected by daily planning and branching events. Use route and scene options to investigate different schedules, discover hidden quests, revisit character interactions, and access alternate outcomes without restarting the entire campaign.

### Combat & Weapons

Bloodcrave is not a conventional combat game. Conflict is presented through dialogue, vampire and vigilante story events, moral decisions, and character tests. The trainer supports these systems by helping with applicable checks and progression requirements rather than modifying weapons, ammunition, health, or enemy encounters.
